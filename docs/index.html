Field guide

How to play

MoveArrow keys or A / D
JumpSpace, Up, or W. Hold for extra height.
GoalCollect coins and reach the red flag.
HazardsStomp enemies. Avoid their sides, spikes, and pits.

Hold a movement button and Jump together on touchscreens. Pause and Restart are above the game. Green pennants mark safe recovery points. Cyan platforms move; orange traps flash before activating. Snow has momentum. Press P or Escape to pause. Unlocked levels and your best score are saved in this browser when storage is available.
